Tartan Track

A face for the Suunto Run 2: AMOLED black with everything in tartan red. The minutes run round a track drawn as lane lines, with a finish line at twelve and the T above the centre. Only thin lines light up, so it's easy on the battery.

Seconds hand

What goes on the watch

Every image the face package carries, at the sizes the Run 2's face format uses. The hands point up and turn around the centre of their image.

Before it goes on

  1. Date and battery are drawn by the watch itself, in its own font. Here they're stand-ins, placed where the face asks for them.
  2. The seconds colour is a choice on the watch, like the Suunto faces: all seven hands ship in the package.
  3. Next, Tartan writes the face's layout file (positions, which image is which hand) and installs it with one tap from Settings, with a check before anything is sent.
