# Tartan

A free training coach for your watch. Tartan reads your workouts (from your watch, Health
Connect, intervals.icu or Strava), writes a running plan around your life, puts it on your watch,
and answers questions about your training. Android 8 or newer.

## Install

The easiest way is [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), which installs Tartan
and keeps it up to date:

1. In Obtainium, tap **Add App**.
2. Paste `https://github.com/sanjinpepic/Tartan` and tap **Add**.
3. Tap **Install**.

Or download the APK from [Releases](https://github.com/sanjinpepic/Tartan/releases/latest).

## Is it really Tartan?

Every release is signed with the same key. Its certificate's SHA-256 fingerprint is:

```
76:68:3E:E0:40:AE:1F:C9:95:50:7F:9A:77:4F:2B:FC:AC:57:6F:AB:F0:63:A5:E6:86:81:29:B3:2E:D7:03:D4
```

AppVerifier (or `apksigner verify --print-certs`) shows it for a downloaded APK.
